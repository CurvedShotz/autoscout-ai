AUTO SCRAPER V1.0 Vision-------------------


-PROBLEM STATEMENT
	Finding used cars online is frustrating because listings are spread across multiple marketplaces, search filters are rigid, and many listings are overpriced, duplicated, or potentially fraudulent.

-SOLUTION
	Autoscraper is an online vehicle listing aggregrator that uses AI to scrape and analyse multiple car listing from various marketplaces, whilst flagging suspicious and odd listings and suggesting the best deals according to the prompt

-TARGET AUDIENCE
	Car buyers looking for the best deal across multiple marketplaces, Car enthusiasts looking for a car with specific qualities, students on a tight budget looking for best value vehicles

-CORE FEATURES
	-Natural language Search
	-AI listing ranking
	-scam detection
	-AI overview
	-saved searches
	-listing comparison

-FUTURE FEATURES
	-vehicle history
	-VIN decoding
